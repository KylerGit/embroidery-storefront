About Thread Point

Small studio energy. Production-ready precision.

Thread Point helps modern brands turn good ideas into pieces people want to wear, keep, and give.

Embroidery should feel intentional, not added on at the last minute.

We work closely with founders, merch teams, and independent designers to make embroidery feel considered from the first thread sample to the final quality check.

Our approach is direct and detail-oriented: honest recommendations, dependable timelines, and finishes that hold up in the real world.

Considered

We match stitch density, thread weight, and placement to the garment instead of forcing a one-size-fits-all process.

Dependable

Clear communication and quality checks keep your order moving from artwork approval through delivery.

Useful

Our recommendations are grounded in how your products will actually be worn, washed, packed, and sold.
